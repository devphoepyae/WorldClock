Privacy Policy

WorldClock

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Applies to WorldClock 1.0.0 for Android (com.worldclock.offline)

WorldClock is built to work entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

The short version We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you add to the app stays in the app’s private storage on your phone.

1. Information we collect

None. WorldClock does not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location data, contacts, or any other information about you. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your setup between launches, WorldClock stores the following locally in its private app storage:

  • The cities you save, together with any labels you give them
  • Your selected theme
  • Your 12-hour / 24-hour time-format preference

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access it. You can remove it at any time by deleting individual cities in the app, clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app.

3. Internet access and third parties

WorldClock does not request the Android INTERNET permission. All times, daylight-saving transitions and day/night shading are calculated on your device using the time-zone data included with Android. The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, social plugins, or other third-party services that collect data.

4. Android system backup

Android’s backup feature is enabled for the app. If you have device backup turned on in Android Settings, the operating system may include WorldClock’s local settings in your own encrypted Google backup, under your Google account and Google’s terms. This is performed by Android, not by WorldClock; we have no access to that backup. You can turn backup off in Android Settings at any time.

5. Permissions

WorldClock requests no sensitive Android permissions. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, files, or advertising ID.

6. Children’s privacy

WorldClock does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for general audiences and contains no advertising or in-app purchases.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data, there is no personal information for us to access, correct, export or delete. The settings stored on your device remain under your control through Android’s app-storage controls and by uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of WorldClock changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the “Last updated” date above will change. The current version of this policy always applies to the current release.
